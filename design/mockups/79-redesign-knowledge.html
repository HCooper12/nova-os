Notes, Journal and Galaxy

The three ways you meet your own vault: read a page, write a day, see how it all connects. Today only one of the six doors between them exists. Every direction keeps every function, gives each screen one object and one colour, and builds the missing doors.

Demo content: every page, title, day, entry, question and star here is invented. The numbers marked "today" were measured in demo mode at 390 by 844 under your look; the audit with all of them is design/audits/redesign-2026-09/15-knowledge.md. The map is a stand-in drawn on this page; the app's own Galaxy keeps its layout engine.
