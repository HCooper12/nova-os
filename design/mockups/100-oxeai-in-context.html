Today on Fuel: The Plate, then the Composer, Log again, and Today's log. Nothing tells you how the day went.

Where it lives: directly under The Plate, from the moment the day's last usual meal is logged (or 7 pm). Before then the plate's own "66 g protein to go" line does the job. On Home it's one line and a small ring from 7 pm, and it opens Fuel.

How: one line in the serif saying the day, then Helped and Held back. Each fact has a coloured tile (protein cyan, calories green, fat amber), its number, and a meter with your target marked. "Show a score" adds the published formula. "Dinner not logged" shows why the reasons version is safer.
