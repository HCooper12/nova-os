The sky's drift, and how pages change

Two small motions that cost your phone more than they show. Each is drawn below with real glass, slowed down where it helps, so you can see what you would be choosing.

1 · The sky's drift

Behind Nova glass, the sky's colours drift about 2.5% over 40 seconds, forever. You can hardly see it. But every glass card and the tab bar has to re-blur whatever moves behind it, every frame, for as long as anything moves: on Home that is seven surfaces covering 73% of the screen while you are simply reading.

Drifting

The sky drifts for five seconds after you touch or scroll, then stops where it is, and the glass stops being redrawn. At real speed you cannot see the moment it stops. Touch this phone to wake it. The colours and the time-of-day shading stay exactly as they are.

What I can't tell you from here is how much your phone feels it: Chrome on a Mac can't measure your iPhone's glass. The quick test is yours: record Home at rest with Calm off, then with Calm on (Calm stops the drift), and compare how smooth the two feel.

2 · How pages change

A correction first. I told you every tab tap photographs the whole page. I checked the code: it doesn't. Since your 17 September fix, a tab tap moves only the page's content and leaves the tab bar live. Two other ways of changing page still use the old method: tapping a card that opens another page, and Nova's own back buttons.

Below, both at one-sixth speed. On the left, a tab tap: the content dips and rises, the tab bar never moves. On the right, a card tap or Back: the browser photographs the whole page, tab bar included, fades the old photo out quickly and the new one in more slowly, so halfway through the bar is two half-faded photos at once. That is the doubling and wash-out you filmed on 17 September.

The option: every page change moves the way a tab tap does. The morphs stay (a recipe card growing into its page, a technique card opening into the book), because those are a different thing: one object travelling, not the whole page.

Your calls

  1. The sky: always drifting, or resting when you doResting is my recommendation: you lose a motion you can barely see, and the phone stops redrawing most of Home while you read. If you would rather keep it, it stays exactly as it is.
  2. Card taps and Back: move like a tab tapMy recommendation, and the same fix you approved for tabs on 17 September. Card taps and Back get the content-only rise, and the tab bar can no longer double or wash out. If you say no, they keep the whole-page photo and its occasional glitch.
