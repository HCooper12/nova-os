The end: C's boards, and verbs that say what they do

When Nova finishes, the five parts lift out of the rail and spread out as boards, each carrying a small picture of what it showed: the reveal you asked to be reminded of. Tap a board to hear that part again; the sixth plays it all from the start. Below them, every verb has its consequence written under it: the first group keeps the information, the second leaves it with you to think over, the third changes something. Discard sits apart, and says it writes nothing.

On the Mac, at the endThe boards spread across the stage in one row, and the end card is a column beside them, so nothing hides in a sheet. While it plays, the stage sits centred at 720 pt and Script opens as a side panel instead of a sheet.
