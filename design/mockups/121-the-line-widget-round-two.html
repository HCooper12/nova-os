He left this to me. With the widget now for reading, a button on the face costs more than it gives: it crowds the attribution and makes a quiet object look like a control. A tap anywhere already opens the line in Nova, where keeping it is one tap. This is how it would have looked.

What gets built, once approved

Real words only
Every line is the speaker’s own, found by code in his vault and checked against the transcript. Nova never writes one.
The lines he likes
The pool leans on the shows and speakers he named, and a line is used only when it has been verified.
One line a day
It changes at midnight and holds all day, chosen by the date so every size agrees. A line due in his review that day goes first.
The time is proof
The timecode shows only when the transcript gives it.
