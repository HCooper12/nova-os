Nova's icon, in motion

The reel gives an assistant a body that changes shape as a conversation moves: a loose sphere while you talk, a turning knot while it thinks, a dense shell while it answers, and every word poured out of it. Below are its moves, named, and three ways to give them to Nova's core, in Nova's own colours.

Demo content: the question and the reply are invented. The voice level here is simulated from paced words; in the app it comes from your microphone while you talk and from Nova's voice while he speaks.

Three ways to give Nova these moves

Each keeps the blue core you know at rest. They differ in how much of it changes while you talk to him.

The reel's moves, named

Times are the reel's own. Show me plays the move on the stage; the quick ones play at quarter speed.

  1. 0:00.0

    The grain sphere

    A loose ball of a few thousand fine grains, faint and slowly turning. It is the whole face; nothing else on the screen moves.

    Listening, in A and B: the core loosens into grains while you talk. Today the rings tint violet.

  2. 0:00.4

    The ink-in

    Each word the person says arrives dim and brightens over a beat. The newest word is the brightest; the earlier ones settle.

    Your words while you talk, in every variation. They rise from below, where your voice comes from.

  3. 0:00.6

    The ripple

    Bands of light travel diagonally across the sphere, quicker and brighter on every syllable.

    Listening, in A and B, driven by your microphone, the bands rising from the bottom edge.

  4. 0:01.6

    The shear

    The sphere cuts into horizontal slices that slide apart in alternate directions, in about a fifth of a second.

    Every change of state. In A and B the grains slice; in C it is the main new move, the core image itself slicing.

  5. 0:01.9

    The knot

    The slices wind into a ribbon tangle that turns slowly, the grains flowing along it, and the colour changes.

    Thinking, in A and B, in Nova's cyan. Today thinking only runs the rings three times faster.

  6. 0:02.5

    The inflate

    The knot drops back into slices and the slices swell into a dense shell, in under half a second.

    Thinking to speaking, in A and B.

  7. 0:02.9

    The membrane

    A dense, soft shell with a lit rim. It bulges and wobbles with the voice, and a band of light slides down it.

    Speaking, in A and B, in the coral-red you kept. In B the heart glows inside it.

  8. 0:03.0

    The pour

    For each word, a plume of grains leaves the underside and lands as that word. The newest is white, the rest settle grey, and the line clears at each new sentence.

    Nova's subtitles in every variation: from the shell in A, the heart in B, the core in C. The word being said keeps its underline, in the speaking colour.

  9. 0:08.8

    The settle

    The shell rounds off, thins out and turns back to the resting colour while the last sentence stays a moment, then fades.

    Back to rest: the grains fall back into the rings around the heart.

  10. Throughout

    The named state

    Three outlined labels above the face, the current one lit in its state's colour.

    Kept as Nova does it now: the state named in words beside the core, so colour is never the only signal.

At every size Nova appears

The same clock drives the tab bar's Nova and the presenter spot in full screen. The pour needs subtitles, so it lives only where they do. At 58 points the knot reads as a tangle rather than a knot; whether it still reads as Nova is for you to judge on your phone.

Your calls

  2. Thinking gets a colour of its own: Nova's cyanIf yes, thinking reads at a glance across the room, as listening and speaking already do. If no, it stays blue and only the shape (A, B) or the speed (C) tells you he is working.

If it is built

  • The grain forms become a third engine inside NovaCore, beside filament and hologram, so every surface gets them from one place and the rest state stays the core you chose in Settings.
  • Grains capped at 1,600 on the phone, one canvas, paused when the screen is hidden. The reel uses 12,000; that many would cost your phone's battery for little you could see at this size.
  • The ripple reads your microphone level and the membrane reads Nova's voice level, both already measured by the app. Word timing stays paced per sentence until the voice engine can report true word times.
  • Reduced motion: each state a still frame, a quarter-second cross-fade between them, the words fading in without the pour.
