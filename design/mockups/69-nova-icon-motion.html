What changed since round 1

  1. Your hologram at rest

    The core you know from the app: tilted rings, comets, the globe, the ember cloud. Round 1 drew the older filament engine by mistake.

  2. The rings shed their grains

    As you start talking, the rings dissolve into the grains that become the sphere, and the heart stays where it is.

  3. A pulse on every word

    A band of light climbs the shell from where each word leaves, so his speech has a rhythm you can see with the sound off.

  4. The shell reaches for the line

    Its underside swells toward the subtitle while a word pours out of it, then settles.

  5. Two rings stay in orbit

    Two of the hologram's rings keep circling the shell while he speaks, so even mid-sentence he is recognisably Nova. The rim is lit lighter than the body, and he draws a small breath at the start of each sentence.

  6. The panel rises out of him

    When there is something to show, a glass panel materialises from the core (blur and scale together), the core travels up to make room, the bar he is talking about lights as he says it, and the panel settles away when he finishes.

  7. Red means he disagrees

    Only the sentence that pushes back turns red, and the label says "Pushing back" so the colour is never the only signal. His reasons return to the speaking colour, and the panel rises with the reason, lighting the evidence as he names it.

The tab bar's Nova and Home's core

Both run on the same clock as the full screen, so talking to Nova from any page plays the same moves. Pours and panels need room, so they stay in full screen. At 36 points the shapes are at their limit; judge them on your phone.
