The Leader, three ways

The Leader keeps a picture of what you are working against, what is working and what you have set down, and you talk it through together. Each direction keeps every function the page has today; what changes is what you meet first, how the picture is drawn, and how a struggle leaves it.

What the audit measured. Ten struggles told since 7 September and none ever resolved: the page has no place to show one resolved, and marking one is two hidden taps 1.2 screens down. The question the Leader is waiting on sits on the fold with its answer box below it; the conversation lives in a 388 pt box inside the page and is gone after a reload; 14 type sizes and five hues, none of them the Leader's own magenta.

Demo content. The team, the struggles, the books, the conversation and every date on this page are invented; nothing comes from your vault, and no agent was asked anything to make it.

Whichever you pick

  • The conversation is kept: after a reload the page reads it back from the Leader's own session instead of showing an empty prompt beside New conversation.
  • Every reply says who wrote it and whom it asked, with each agent's question, its time and its own words, from the roster the job already records.
  • What you set down stays in the picture with the day it ended, and Undo sits where it landed, not in the Inbox history.
  • One composer with the mic. While the Leader's question waits, what you send answers it; one tap clears that to talk about something else.
  • The two channels stay apart: the day's idea is about leading in general and is never written from the open picture.
  • Every control is 44 pt, four type sizes a view at most, your words at 17. Magenta means the Leader; red and gold are gone from the page.
  • Nothing nags: one question at a time, never a second while one waits, as today.
