The same quiet face, now with a life of its own. The light in it travels with the day, one phrase in each line carries the weight, a tap turns the line over, and the three looks become a setting.

A widget cannot play a film. iOS redraws it at set moments and blends from one drawing to the next, so every movement on this page is one of those: four through the day, one at midnight, one after a tap.

What gets built, once approved

Real words only
Every line is the speaker’s own, found by code in his vault and checked against the transcript. Nova never writes one.
The phrase
Chosen once, when a line joins the pool. Code checks it is an exact run of words from the line, and drops it if not.
One line a day
It changes at midnight and holds all day, chosen by the date so every size agrees. The words are always shown.
Through the day
Four drawings: sunrise, midday, evening, night. iOS blends from one to the next.
The look
Follows the day, Nova, Ink or Paper, set on the widget.
The dots
Turn the line over to its idea and back. A tap anywhere else opens that line in Nova. Reading never marks anything as reviewed.
Speaker unknown
The widget names the show and stops there. The timecode shows only when the transcript gives it.
Sizes
Small, medium, large and the lock-screen rectangle, as a fifth widget beside Rings, One number, Fuel and Today.
