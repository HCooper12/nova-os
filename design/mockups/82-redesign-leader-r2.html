Every function, where it lives

  • Today's idea, with why and its sourcesBlend 1: the Now card in the morning, a row the rest of the day; Why this opens in place. Blend 2: the top card in the morning, one card back after.
  • Earlier ideas (the trail)Both: up the conversation, as A drew them: each morning's idea is the Leader's message that morning, and each opens to its why and sources.
  • The Leader's question, answered typed or spoken, and Say moreBoth: first once the morning is over; the one composer with the mic answers it, and sending again before you leave adds to the same answer.
  • The picture: every struggle and win with its ageBoth: the strip of beads at the top; a tap raises A's stage (round 1, frame A2) with each thread drawn over time.
  • Set it down, with UndoBoth: by answering the question, by a tick in the round-up, or from the stage. The bead ties off in the strip and the receipt with Undo stays where it happened.
  • Still openBoth: the round-up's cross; its bead is marked as checked today.
  • The round-upBlend 1: opens in place of the Now card. Blend 2: its cards join the same deck. First when the picture has been quiet three days and no question waits.
  • The conversation and New conversationBlend 1: rises as a sheet with the item quoted. Blend 2: fills the page when the deck is empty. Kept across reloads in both; New conversation is in the menu.
  • Whom the Leader asked, and their own wordsBoth: seats in each agent's colour while they work, then the byline and a row per agent under the reply, with what it was asked and how long it took.
  • The research count and last runBoth: behind the menu, as in round 1.
  • The honest line, and the follow-up's restraintBoth: under the strip, in the Leader's voice; never a second question while one waits, and the round-up held the same way.

Your calls

  1. Blend 1 or Blend 2Blend 1: one Now card with the rest as rows you can see at a glance. Blend 2: one card at a time, the rest behind it. Either way the order rule and every function stay; your pick comes back drawn at 1280 as well, and nothing is built until you say the page is done.
  2. Morning before a waiting questionYes, as drawn: until your first meeting, today's idea is first and the question second. No: a waiting question is always first, and the idea sits second even at 07:00.
  3. Where the morning endsAt your first work block, as drawn, read the same way the Summary Home already reads them. Or at a fixed time such as 11:00, which needs no calendar and is the same every day.
  4. A reply you have not read goes firstYes: a small new "seen" mark on the Leader's replies, and the reply leads even in the morning (the Thursday column). No: it waits in the conversation with a dot on its row, and the order is otherwise unchanged.
  5. Home's Leader box shows the same first thingYes: Home calls the same rule, so the two can never disagree about what matters now. No: Home keeps its two faces, the idea and the situation, as today.
