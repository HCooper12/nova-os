Mentors: Nova studies the people you choose

You pick a person. Nova finds their videos, podcasts and books, reads every transcript, writes what it learned into your vault, keeps watching for new releases, and uses it across the app. What it learned becomes a playbook you can open in the Library.

The playbook text is the real output of today's pilot: 4 Jefferson Fisher videos, read and woven. Anything else carries a sample tag. Tap things: the frames work.

1The Library gets a Mentors shelf

A new Mentors chip sits beside Books, Videos, Podcasts and Articles. Behind it, the people you follow and their playbooks.

The chips are the Library's real ones, with Mentors added at the end. The rest of the Library works as today.

Each person is a card showing how much of their catalogue Nova has read. Today that is 4 of Jefferson Fisher's 235 long videos, and the line under it says why it stopped.

The rail shows part of the next card, so you can see there is more. Here the next card is Follow someone.

His playbook follows as its own rail, one cover per chapter, each with the small shape of its diagram. Tap a cover to open the playbook on that chapter.

Nothing to type. You tap Follow on a name Nova already suggests, or share a channel link. Nova lists what it found and you check it is the right person.

4The playbook

A document you page through: at a glance first, then a chapter per method, then the scripts, revision cards, his guests and every source. Swipe sideways or use the arrows; Contents jumps anywhere.

Every word here is the pilot's, from the vault pages Nova wrote today. 8 chapters, 77 rules each with a source and minute, 61 phrases found word for word in the transcripts.

A chapter reads top to bottom: the summary, three takeaways, the diagram, the method as a joined path, when to use it, the rules with their minute, phrases to reuse, mistakes, and where it stops applying.

Watch the minute opens the real video at the real second. The minute chip beside any rule does the same.

Scripts put what to say beside what you would have said, filtered by situation. Revision cards turn over when you tap them; Got it or Again.

6News

A new page where Nova reports what changed in the things it watches for you, written like news. It starts with the people you follow and can carry more later.

Home gets one quiet line, in the feed with everything else. The greeting and the cards around it are sample.

No push. Your notification rule stands: News is a page you open, and Home gets a single line when there is something new.

The weekly piece says what Nova learned and what it filed. Nova files on its own, so the Undo for the whole week sits right on it.

A guest spot asks first the first time for each show: Is this him? After a yes, that show's appearances are read without asking.

A book gets noticed and Nova asks for the file, since it reads books deeply only from files you own.
