Mentors: Nova studies the people you choose

You pick a person. Nova finds their videos, podcasts and books, reads every transcript, writes what it learned into your vault, keeps watching for new releases, and uses it across the app. What it learned becomes a playbook you can open in the Library.

The playbook text is the real output of today's pilot: 4 Jefferson Fisher videos, read and woven. The 235 videos on the map are his real channel. Anything else carries a sample tag. Tap things: the frames work.

One colour language for where work is at, the same on every screen, and never colour alone:

    1The Library gets a Mentors shelf

    A new Mentors chip sits beside Books, Videos, Podcasts and Articles. Behind it, the people you follow and their playbooks.

    The chips are the Library's real ones, with Mentors added at the end.

    Each person carries a small map of their catalogue, one dot per long video. Jefferson Fisher has 235: 4 are woven (mint), 1 was refused by YouTube (amber), and the rest are found and waiting.

    The line under it says why it stopped, in the waiting colour with its clock.

    Each chapter cover draws its own diagram small, from the parts Nova wrote for it, so the eight covers look different at a glance. The rail lets the next cover peek.

      4The playbook

      A field guide you page through. Each page leads with a short glance layer, and everything else opens in place.

      Glance or Full, in the header. Glance is the default and shows each page's short version. Full opens everything on the page. The choice holds as you turn pages.

      Page 1 is a ledger: the seven principles, each with four dots for how many of the 4 videos it came up in. Tap one for its sentence and the minute to watch.

      A chapter opens with its number, title and purpose, then its diagram, then three takeaways. Below, one row per kind of content with its count: the method, when to use it, rules, phrases, mistakes, where it stops applying, and the minute to watch.

      The kinds keep one colour: phrases to say are mint with quote marks, mistakes coral with a cross, where it stops amber with an edge. Rules stay neutral and carry their source and minute.

      The bar at the bottom is the book's shape: at a glance, the eight chapters, practice, then guests and sources. The playbook keeps his own colour, gold. Nova's cyan is only for things you can tap.

      Try

      • Glance, then Full
      • A principle on page 1
      • Contents, then any chapter
      • The situations on Scripts
      • A revision card

      The same playbook on the Mac, in Full: contents down the side, the page beside it.

      6News

      News gets its own build run and its own mockup. This section is a sketch and is not part of this approval.

      A new page where Nova reports what changed in the things he watches for you, written like news. It starts with the people you follow and can carry more later.

      Home gets one quiet line, in the feed with everything else. The greeting and the cards around it are sample.

      No push. Your notification rule stands: News is a page you open, and Home gets a single line when there is something new.

      The weekly piece says what Nova learned and what it filed. Nova files on its own, so the Undo for the whole week sits right on it.

      A guest spot asks first the first time for each show: Is this him? After a yes, that show's appearances are read without asking.

      A book gets noticed and Nova asks for the file, since it reads books deeply only from files you own.
