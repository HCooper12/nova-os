Nova on your wrist

A native Apple Watch app built around the moments your phone is in your bag or your hands are full: lifting, eating, a thought you want kept, and a glance at where the day stands. Everything that needs reading, typing or a big screen stays on the phone.

Sample content only. No number, lift or food on this page is yours.

1 · the hero

The session

The watch carries the workout so the phone can stay in the bag. One lift on screen, one target, one tick. The first watch is playable: tap the green tick.

2 · the glance

Today, food and your voice

Three short screens, reached by turning the crown from the first one. Each does one thing.

3 · widgets

On the watch face and in the Smart Stack

Complications are full colour on the watch, so each number keeps its hue. They read the same feed your phone widgets use.

4 · when things are missing

It says so

The watch reaches your Mac through your iPhone. When a link in that chain is down, the screen says which one and what happens to your taps.

how it reaches home

The path every tap takes

Your watch is not on your private network, and should not need to be. The phone already is.

WatchHolds today's plan and your usuals. Keeps taps in a queue when alone.
iPhonePasses each tap on in the background. The Nova app does not need to be open.
Your MacTested code writes to the vault. Every write is undoable, as now.

on the wrist

What is in

  • The session: ticks, rest, changes by crown, heart rate, a real Health workout
  • Today: three rings and the next session
  • Usuals: one-tap food log with Undo
  • Voice: a short answer, or a note kept
  • Four complications and two Smart Stack cards

stays on the phone

What is out, and why

  • Journal, Library, briefings, research: reading and writing at length
  • Recipes, shopping, new foods by search: lists and typing
  • The 3D figure, Agent World, the living stage: need the screen
  • Coach's proposed changes: a decision deserves the full card. A later round could bring a tick and cross for the simple ones.
  • Settings: set once, on the phone
