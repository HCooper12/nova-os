The other eight pages, on the MacBook

The window

The sky runs to the window's edge. Pages sit on twelve columns inside the sidebar; text holds near 66 characters. Three widths, read from the page's own room: under 900, 900 to 1239, 1240 and up.

The sidebar is the parent

No "back to Index" on the Mac. Sentence case, a real icon on every row, your order, counts only from what the page itself shows, the lit row in the page's own colour. Agents live on the wall and in Ops.

Sheets become panes

What rises from the foot on the phone slides in from the right on the Mac, 400 wide, with no dimming, and leaves the way it came. Only a destructive confirm stays a centred dialog.

Keys on every page

Hover only for a pointer

A row tints and shows its quiet second verb; a card never lifts. Tooltips carry the key. Nothing depends on hover: every verb is also a key and a visible button.

Motion
