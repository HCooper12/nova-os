To-Do, Shopping and Stash

Three screens with one job: put a thing on a list, see it grouped, then finish it or let it go. Today each draws that job its own way. Here it is one grammar in three colours, three ways.

Demo content. Every to-do, item, recipe, link, shelf, time and count here is invented. The audit with the measurements is design/audits/redesign-2026-09/13-lists.md.

Each screen's colour

None of the three has a hue today: the Index gives all three the neutral ink, and inside, Shopping and Stash wear gold on their titles and every heading. A proposal from Nova's own tokens, each with a reason. Gold keeps one job here, a to-do that has waited on you; red keeps one, remove.

To-Do · violet--nv-vi

The one place Nova already colours your to-do world is the Todoist sync, in violet. It sits farthest from the two colours a to-do must keep: gold for waiting and red for remove.

Shopping · green--nv-good

The list is fed by Fuel (a recipe adds its lines), and the Index already gives food green. Here green is also "got it", which is the same act.

Stash · teal--nv-m-back

The Librarian's hue: "files every note where you will find it again". That is the Stash's whole job. It is also the back muscle's hue, which only ever appears in Train.

The four states, designed

Today a load in flight shows "Loading…" on two screens and "Connect a backend in Settings" plus "Nothing on the list yet" on Shopping; offline, Shopping still says live.

On the Mac, for the desktop round

Measured at 1280 today, the three lists are three widths (780, 962 and 820 pt cards), and Shopping's stepper sits about 650 pt from the item it changes. Nothing here answers Command-Z.

A and B would hold one reading width for all three (about 680 pt), with the hover and keyboard of a Mac list: arrow keys move, Space ticks, Delete removes, Command-Z undoes. C would set the three lists side by side as three columns, so the whole of your lists is one glance.
