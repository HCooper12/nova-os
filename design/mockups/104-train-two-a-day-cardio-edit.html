How it behaves

  • Two workouts on one day: Begin on that day asks which one first. The second one starts as its own session, so each keeps its own history.
  • "N of 6 done" counts each planned workout. A day with two workouts counts as two.
  • A walk counts only when it is at least 15 minutes long. Shorter walks still show, but don't tick the day.
  • Renaming a routine keeps all of its past sessions and records under the new name.
  • Nothing here needs Claude: every edit is a direct save to your vault, just like a normal workout app.
