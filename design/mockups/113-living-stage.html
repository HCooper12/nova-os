What changes, and why

  • The window is his while he holds the floor. The page rail leaves when Nova listens or speaks and returns at rest. Train, Fuel and the rest stay one tap from rest.
  • One panel per sentence, and they stay. Each lands as its sentence starts. By the end the whole briefing is up at once.
  • No cap, placed by rule. A new panel takes the free spot nearest the core that overlaps nothing, with the two sides kept even. When the window is full, the oldest one folds into Earlier, which counts them and opens them all.
  • Sized by kind. A figure is small. A session, a video or a proposal is larger. Nothing is padded to fill a cell.
  • Colour means the subject. Each muscle keeps his own hue, gold is fuel and what waits on him, red is what is missing, green is a change he can approve.
  • Agents are parts of the one Nova. A lobe is a running job in that agent's hue. A line ties each panel to the agent that made it.
  • Work ends in his word. A tick, a cross, one "Do all", or saying it. Every decision has Undo.
  • Reduced motion swaps every move for a short fade.
