The wall, with your agents

A's quiet wall, and C's agents when they need you. Two ways to blend them, each acted out with nobody waiting, one agent asking, and several.

"Id like a refinement of some way for both A and C as I like the simple view of a but I like the option to see the agents and anything they need my attention for from c"You, 7 October, on round 1 (mockup 81)

Demo content: every ask, count and receipt is invented, and so is every agent's state. The faces and the core are flat stand-ins for the 3D ones in the app.

Records say

Blend 1 · Hands up

At rest it is A. When an agent needs you, its face rises beside the gold count with a numbered badge; when one is working, it circles the core. Tap the count and a sheet rises with each agent's asks, who is working, and all ten. Nobody appears on the wall without a record that says so.

Blend 2 · Second page

The glance is A, unchanged. The gold count names who is asking, and the second page dot turns gold. One swipe is all ten agents: whoever asks first, large, with its asks, and a strip of every face. On the Mac the org sits beside the glance, because there is room.

Where Console lives: Home, in the morning

The same answer as A in round 1, for either blend. The wall's job is who needs you; the instruments live where you already look each morning. The Console route and its Index row go, and the glass still raises the instruments when the morning brief is spoken.

Where each thing comes from

Nothing on the wall is a constant. When a source cannot say, the wall says nothing, or says so in words.

The gold countorgMap.waitingTotal: every inbox record with status pending. The same records ops.pending counts, so the count and the faces cannot disagree.
A face beside it, and its badgeorgMap.beings[i].waiting: that agent's own pending records, matched by kind. Most first.
Each ask in the sheetorgMap.beings[i].asks, its three newest pending records, then "N more". Your own captures are orgMap.core (Yours to sort); kinds with no agent yet are orgMap.unfiled, counted and named, never dropped.
Circling the core, "Working now"workingAgentNames() in src/vals/agentsWorking.js: a job in flight on this device, or a record still classifying on the rails. Offline and demo say nothing; nobody working says nothing.
An agent's lineAs valsOrgMap.beingLine writes it: waiting, working, then how its loops are doing (ran today, recent, gone quiet, never run). Quiet agents fade by the same rule.
No signalops absent: no gold, no faces, no orbit, grey words.

Kept from round 1, in both blends

  • A findable way out.A tap shows Done, Dim and Agents; Done, Esc or a swipe down returns you to the page you came from. A bump never ends the wall, and Back never brings it back.
  • Fits every screen.Upright, on a stand and on the MacBook; nothing below the edge, nothing scrolls sideways.
  • Gold means waiting.Only the count, the badges, the light along the foot and the page dot. All clear is quiet ink; no signal is grey, with words.
  • A cheaper core.104 points, 30 frames, still when dimmed; the hidden tab-bar orb stops; faces are still portraits, so no WebGL on the wall; the wall drifts a few points an hour.
  • Reduced motion holds still.Faces cross-fade in place, the orbit stops where it is, the sheet fades, the colon holds.
  • Accessible.Every face is a labelled button, the count is a live region, each instrument on the Home card has a VoiceOver label for every bar, block and band.
