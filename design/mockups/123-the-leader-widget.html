What it is made of

The words
The day's idea exactly as the Leader wrote it: its kind, its title, how to do it, why, and the source it drew on. The widget adds no words of its own.
Never on it
The live work situation, names from it, or anything from the Leader's private picture.
Medium, front
The first sentence of how to do it. The rest is one tap away in Nova and whole on the large size.
Looks
Follows the day, Nova, Ink or Paper, chosen by holding the widget, the same as The Line.
Movement
Only what iOS allows a widget: the light moves four times a day, the new idea blends in each morning, and the turn-over blends.
