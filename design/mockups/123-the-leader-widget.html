Two widgets in one stack. Swipe up and down between today's leadership idea and your situation at work. Tap the idea and it turns over to why it works.

Everything on this page is sample content. The built widgets show only what the Leader really wrote.

What it is made of

The idea
The day's idea exactly as the Leader wrote it: its kind, title, how to do it, why, and the source it drew on. Nothing about your situation, so the advice stays general.
Your situation
The thread the Leader is following, the day you last told Nova about it, counted by code, and the Leader's one question. Its own widget, so the two never mix.
Privacy
Your situation is hidden while the phone is locked and shows once you unlock. The idea shows either way.
Taps
The idea: a tap turns it over, a tap on the back opens Nova on it. Your situation: a tap opens Nova on its question.
Looks
Follows the day, Nova, Ink or Paper, chosen by holding the widget, the same as The Line.
Movement
Only what iOS allows a widget: the light moves four times a day, a new idea blends in each morning, the turn-over blends, and the stack slides.
