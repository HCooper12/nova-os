    Rehearse a conversation

    The Leader plays the other person and stays in character, out loud, every turn. The sheet arrives already filled in from the thread and your Practice page. Change anything, or just start.

    Under each thing you say it marks the move you used, in your own words. Ask the Leader pauses the scene for one line of advice.

    The debrief comes once, at the end: your strongest line, what landed, one thing to try next time, and what the other person was holding back.

    Nothing said in a rehearsal is taken as news about work, so the string gets a mark but does not tighten. No streaks, no reminders, no daily prompt. It is there when you want it.

      What it is made of

      Hearing it
      When something you say anywhere in Nova is about work, a cheap model reads it once and says what is new, what is easing and what is done. Code checks each item against what you actually said, writes it, and keeps an Undo.
      The threads
      The Leader groups your notes into threads, and again when a new one arrives. Code stores which note belongs where.
      The strings
      Code draws each string from the dates in the record. The slack is the count of days since you last raised the thread, and the colour comes from the same count. Nothing about a string is a model's opinion.
      The tidy-up
      Code picks the threads quiet for over 30 days; the Leader only words the question. Nothing is set down without your tick.
      What it reads
      Mid-answer, the Leader can ask your Practice pages, the Researcher or Coach, and the answer names what it read.
      Rehearsals
      They run on the Practice rails Nova already has. The moves come from your Practice page. After every turn the Leader names the move you used and quotes you; code checks each name against the page before it shows. The debrief is saved to the page with Undo. What the other person was holding back never reaches the screen until the debrief.
      Ideas and drills
      The Leader argues against the idea first, then rewrites it, and nothing is kept on a thread unless you tap to keep it. In a drill, code counts the words from what you said.
      No nagging
      Rehearsals and drills never send a reminder, keep a streak or ask daily.
      The daily idea
      Stays general and separate. Threads feed the Leader's questions about your situation, never the daily idea.
      Privacy
      The threads live on your Mac in the Leader's own record. The situation widget stays hidden on the lock screen.
