The dots open into what the Leader knows

LeaderWhat I know about work

On the Leader page today, your 18 problems are 18 blank dots, so you can't see what the Leader believes.

The dots become a short list of threads, each one thing going on at work, with when you last mentioned it. The Leader groups what you've said into threads, and code keeps the list.

Tap a thread to see your own words as the Leader wrote them down. Then That's done, Not right anymore (say what is), or Talk about this, which opens the Leader with that thread quoted.

A thread you haven't mentioned in a month shows its age, and the Leader asks before treating it as current.

    What it is made of

    Hearing it
    When something you say in any of Nova's places is about work, a cheap model reads it once and says what is new, what is easing and what is done, in your words. Code checks that each item is a sentence you actually said something about, applies it, and keeps an Undo.
    The threads
    The Leader groups the problems into threads once and again when a new one arrives; code stores which problem belongs to which. A thread's age is the date you last mentioned it, counted by code.
    The tidy-up
    Code picks the threads you haven't mentioned in 30 days; the Leader only words the question. Nothing is set down without your tick.
    The daily idea
    Still general and separate. The threads feed the Leader's questions about your situation, never the daily idea, so the September problem that filled every idea can't happen again.
    Privacy
    The threads live on your Mac in the Leader's own record. The situation widget stays hidden on the lock screen.
