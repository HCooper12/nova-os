    What it is made of

    Hearing it
    When something you say anywhere in Nova is about work, a cheap model reads it once and says what is new, what is easing and what is done. Code checks each item against what you actually said, writes it, and keeps an Undo.
    The threads
    The Leader groups your notes into threads, and again when a new one arrives. Code stores which note belongs where and counts each thread's quiet days from the date you last raised it.
    The tidy-up
    Code picks the threads quiet for over 30 days; the Leader only words the question. Nothing is set down without your tick.
    The daily idea
    Stays general and separate. Threads feed the Leader's questions about your situation, never the daily idea.
    Privacy
    The threads live on your Mac in the Leader's own record. The situation widget stays hidden on the lock screen.
