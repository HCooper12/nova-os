This week, three ways

Mockup 105. All three show your real week of 26 October. My guess at what feels off now: seven identical empty boxes with no words, so you can't tell Pull from Legs or a rest day from a training day without tapping, and "0 of 7 done" counts Tuesday's rest as a job. Each option fixes that differently. Point at the one that feels right, or at pieces of each.

A · The week as a list

Every day says what it is. Today is lifted out with its Begin button. Rest and optional days read quieter. Tap a row to plan that day (the sheet from before). It's the tallest of the three.

Same in all three

  • Tapping a day opens the planning sheet you approved (tick one or more workouts).
  • The count is sessions, not days: a rest day isn't a job, a double day counts twice, and a walk on active rest adds one.
  • Optional days (Arms, Delts and Back) read quieter, so missing one doesn't look like a failure.
  • A day with two workouts shows both names (A), two half circles (B) or a two-colour strip (C).

My pick

A. It's the only one where you can read the whole week at a glance without tapping, and it fits what you check most: what's today and what's next. The cost is height: it's about twice as tall as today's card.
