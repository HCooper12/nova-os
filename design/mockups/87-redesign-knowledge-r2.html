Notes, Journal and Galaxy, round 2

You asked what these three are for. In one line each: Notes is the reader for every page in your vault, the Journal is the day as it was told, and the Galaxy is how the pages connect. Below is what that means in the code, with your vault's real counts, then the three screens redrawn so the job shows at a glance.

The counts in Part 1 were read from your vault on 7 October with the server's own reader, read-only: counts by folder, by kind and by the code's own labels, never a title or a word of an entry. Everything inside the phones is demo content, invented and labelled. Round 1 is mockup 79; its audit is design/audits/redesign-2026-09/15-knowledge.md.

    Part 2 · Journal

    The day, told: Nova's account, then yours

    Part 1 found the Journal is almost entirely Nova and the Coach. So round 2 stops pretending otherwise: a day is its hours, every entry says who wrote it, and your line closes the day in the same composer Notes uses.

    Today is a rail of hours A's track, turned upright

    Each entry is a bead at its time, named by who wrote it: Nova in starlight, the Coach in his coral. The authors come from the labels the code already writes ("Morning dispatch", "Session receipt").

    Your line closes the day C's Today card

    The composer sits at the foot of today's rail as an open bead. Saved, your words land on the rail at the minute you wrote them, in the serif, larger than anything Nova filed.

    Past days keep their shape kept from today

    A spoken date and a 24-hour strip with one tick per entry, coloured by author, so a week of your own words would show at a glance. Opening a day lists its entries in the order of the ticks.

    Doors out fixes round 1 finding 1

    A reflection opens the page it was about, in Notes. An entry from an agent opens the Inbox record it came from, where Undo lives.

    Who and kind, and a filter that stays Who is new

    The filter is one button. Kind keeps today's four (all, personal, training, system); Who is new. An empty result says so and keeps the filter in view, never "No journal entries yet".
