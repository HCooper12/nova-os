On his iPhone · 390

Who plays. Only the agent on the centre slab, and only after the arc has settled. A play fires every 7 to 12 seconds, never two at once, never while you drag, and never while his sheet is open.

Behind and in front. His layer sits either in front of the glass or behind it. Behind, you see him through the panel's frost, which is what makes the peek read as hiding.

Tap him and he reacts in character (the Coach hops, the Librarian ducks, the Leader nods). A tap on him never opens or swipes. If he is working, a tap only turns his head toward you and the line says what he is doing.

Open. He hops onto the bottom edge of the rising sheet and rides it up, then stands at its top while you read. Back lowers the sheet and he steps back down to his slab along the same path.

These buttons are for this review only; in Nova a play is never triggered by a control.

The plays, and who does which

Jump in front

Two or three hops at the foot of the slab, squashing on each landing.

Hide and peek

Slips behind, crosses under the frost, leans out from the far edge, then goes back.

Round the back

Walks off one side, passes behind the glass, and comes round the other.

Lean on it

Walks to the edge and leans his weight on the glass, then pushes off.

Sit on top

One big leap to the top edge, sits with his feet over the front, hops down.

Peek over the top

From behind, rises until only his eyes clear the top edge, looks both ways, sinks.

His round 1 word was "under". The glass stands on the floor, so going under would mean a slab that floats; I drew round the back instead, which keeps the floor honest and still passes him behind the panel. If he wants under, the slabs lift a hand's height and his duck becomes a crawl.

Suggested skills

Inside each agent's sheet, under his skills, Nova proposes skills that agent does not have, each with the evidence that raised it: how many times, over what span, and where the count was read. A tick puts it on the build list as "not yet"; it never grants the skill, because only shipped code can. A cross retires it and the same evidence cannot raise it again for 60 days (the rule respectTheNo.js already applies to the autonomy ladder). Open any agent above to try one. Performance

One WebGL canvas per screen, one live being in it. The other nine are portraits rendered once at load from the same kit and kept as images. The live being renders at 30 fps (the Org map's own cap) only while he is playing, reacting, riding or working; at rest the loop stops and the last frame stays. Pixel ratio is capped at 2 and no shadow map is used (a soft contact disc instead). The slabs still move by transform and opacity only. Budget: under 8 ms a frame for the being on an iPhone at 30 fps, under 4 ms on the Mac. Not yet measured on his phone; the build records it with the motion-check harness before it ships.

Your calls

While you read an agent's skill set, should he stay standing at the top of the sheet, or hop off and go back to his slab?
Stay: the sheet has a host who blinks and looks at what you tick. Hop off: a quieter sheet. I recommend he stays, still and blinking, with no plays while you read.
When you tick a suggested skill, should it go straight onto the build list, or wait for you to talk it over with Nova first?
Straight on: one tap, and it shows as "not yet" in that agent's list. Talk first: it opens a conversation with the evidence. I recommend straight on, with talking it over one tap away, the same way the Coach's cards work.
Should going under the panel be added, which means lifting the glass a little off the floor?
Yes: he can crawl under, and the slabs float like the reel's. No: the slabs stand on the floor and he goes round the back. I recommend no; round the back gets the same hide without making every slab hover.
