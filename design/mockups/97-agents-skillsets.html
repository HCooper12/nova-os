Every agent, and what each can really do

Ten glass slabs on a lit floor, one per agent, in the Org map's order and each agent's own hue. Swipe to turn the arc. Tap a slab and it breaks into light that gathers into that agent's face, then its full skill set rises underneath.

Every skill shown is in the code today (sources in the HTML comments). Gold DEMO marks a live value drawn here as an example; the note under it names where the real one is read.

On his iPhone · 390

Drag follows the finger 1:1, release carries the flick's speed into a projection, and the arc lands on the slab nearest where it was heading. Past the first or last slab the arc resists and springs back.

The lit slab takes its agent's hue on its edge, the floor's glow and the page dots. Nothing else is coloured, so the hue means "this one".

Working now is the pulsing dot, and it obeys agentsWorking.js: a classifying record of that agent's kinds under 30 minutes old, or a job of its in flight on this device. Otherwise the slab says when it last ran, or "Never run".

Open: slab dissolves (up to 700 particles on a phone), the reticle locks, the particles gather into the face and turn once, the sheet rises. Back plays it in reverse along the same path.

Performance

Slabs move by transform and opacity only, ten layers, six visible. The backdrop blur is paid once per slab, not per frame of motion, because the blur layer is not re-rasterised under a pure transform. Particles: one 2D canvas, at most 700 points on a phone and 1,100 on a Mac, each a 2 px fillRect: roughly 1,100 fillRects a frame, a budget of under 2 ms. Not yet measured on his iPhone; the build must record it there. The canvas exists only during the 1.3 s open and the reverse; at rest there is no loop. Reduce motion removes the canvas entirely.

Your calls

When you tap an agent, should the light gather into his 3D face from the Org map, or into just his object (the Coach's bar, the CFO's coin)?
Face: you meet the same character you know from the Org map, turning once. Object: lighter and faster, but the agent loses his face here. I recommend the face, since you chose faces in 3D.
Should this be a new "Agents" row in More, or live inside the Org map?
A More row: one tap from the Index, and the Org map stays about what is waiting on you. Inside the map: one fewer place, but a busier map. I recommend the More row, with a tap on a being in the map opening the same skill sheet.
Should skills that aren't built yet appear in an agent's list, greyed and marked "not yet"?
Shown: you see what's coming and what's missing. Hidden: the list is only what works today. I recommend showing them, only inside the open list and never on the slab.
Should the carousel turn slowly by itself when you aren't touching it?
Yes: it feels alive like the reel, but moves when nothing has changed. No: it stays still until you swipe. I recommend no; movement should mean something happened.
