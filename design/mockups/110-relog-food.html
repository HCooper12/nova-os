4 · One tap, a tick, the plate moves, Undo. The sheet stays open, so a breakfast of three is three taps or one "Log all". The receipt's new totals are the plate's own numbers.

What changes under it (code, not looks)

  • A food logged to a past day keeps the time he gave it, or the time he logged it, instead of midnight, so "newest first" is true.
  • Portions fold by the food's name with its portion stripped ("(4× serving)", "(1 piece)"); the chip logs that portion's own macros, as logged before.
  • Groups are entries logged within ten minutes of each other on the same day.
  • Search runs over everything in the last 90 days on the phone, so it works offline.

What it removes

  • The "Everything you've logged" door and its Log / Save pair on every row (Save moves to a long-press on a row).
  • Likely now as its own section (its "around this hour" signal becomes the "usual now" mark).
