6 · Logged: a tick, the plate moves, and the amount stays changeable. The receipt carries the amount: tap "½ ▾" to reopen the tray and change it, or Undo. So the one-tap log from the rail can still become 12 or ½ after the fact.

What changes under it (code, not looks)

  • Amounts use Nova's existing portion code (the fractions a recipe already offers, and the same rounding). A count food is one whose name carries a count, like "(1 crisp)", "(1 piece)" or "(3 balls)"; its macros are divided down to one, multiplied up, and the name is rewritten: "Pringles Salt & Vinegar (12 crisps)". Anything else is servings: "Easy Meal Prep Lasagna (½ serving)".
  • The amount you used last becomes that food's default, so once you log 12 crisps a single tap on the rail logs 12.
  • Changing the amount after a log edits that same entry; Undo still takes it off.
  • The same food logged many times in one day (your ten crisps on 19 Oct) reads as one row with its total.
  • A food logged to a past day keeps the time you gave it, or the time you logged it, instead of midnight, so "newest first" is true.
  • Groups are entries logged within ten minutes of each other on the same day. Search runs over the last 90 days on the phone, so it works offline.

What it removes

  • Likely now as its own row (the rail takes its slot; its "around this hour" idea is the "usual now" mark).
  • The "Everything you've logged" door at the foot of Fuel and the Log / Save pair on every row (Save moves to the hold menu).
