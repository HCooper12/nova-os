Whichever you pick

  • The page clears the top bar at the house height, Close returns to where you came from, and Back works because a briefing always gets its own place in history.
  • Every control is 44 pt, four type sizes a view at most, and no 8 pt mono labels.
  • Colour means one thing each: jade only while Nova speaks, terms in ink with "in plain words", the Researcher's blue on what the Researcher found with the words "from the Researcher", gold only for what is still undecided.
  • A panel only when there is something to show: a picture, a clip, a term, a figure, or two things compared. Every figure comes from the research and code checks it before it is drawn.
  • While it is being made, the angles are drawn going out from Nova and coming back as each one finishes, with today's plain words. A failure says what went wrong and offers Try again. Loading shows the stage, not a sentence.
  • Read stays: the document, the terms and the sources, one tap away.
  • Reduced motion: a sentence appears whole with a quarter-second fade, panels fade without rising, Nova holds still.
