The Library

The shelf stays the object. Everything around it shrinks: eight pills become a title row, the page opens on what Nova made of a source, and a source is the same bound volume wherever you meet it. Three ways to do that.

Demo content: every title, channel, author, verdict and count here is invented, and the poster art is abstract colour. The shape (21 videos, 3 articles, 1 book) is your shelf's. The volumes are drawn in CSS 3D as a stand-in for the app's WebGL shelf, which keeps its light. The audit with the numbers is design/audits/redesign-2026-09/09-library.md.

The same in all three

The detail is one design whichever way you arrive. What it changes, against what the audit measured today:

  • Today the detail opens on a badge, a date, an echo count and a note; then three pills, four rows of chips and a related rail.Here it opens on the volume, its name, Nova's verdict in the serif, and two verbs: Ask about it, Open source.
  • Nova's reading starts 1,399 px down (1.66 screens).It starts inside the first screen and a half, with a talk's key ideas placed on its own timeline.
  • 21 controls, 14 of them under the 28 pt floor (the chips are 26 pt).One figure of what it connects to, five counts that open lists, every control 44 pt.
  • Back leaves the Library and the book stays open behind you.Back, or a swipe from the left edge, returns the book to its place, at any point in the flight.
  • One source, four drawings: plate in cloth, the 3D volume, a full-bleed 16:10 poster, a hash gradient.One bound volume everywhere, the same cloth, plate and foil.
  • "25 sources · live from Obsidian", even when it is a cached copy."25 sources · synced 9:38", or "offline, Friday 18:20's copy".
  • Gold for adding, for researched, for concepts, for a heading and for an error; people violet and topics cyan, unlike the Galaxy.The Galaxy's own colours: concepts tan, people coral, topics violet, sources cyan. Gold is left for "not decided".
  • Ten type sizes in three families.34 · 20 · 16 · 13, the serif only for Nova's verdict.
