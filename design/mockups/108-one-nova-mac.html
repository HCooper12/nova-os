What the Mac does differently, and why

  • Panels dock beside the core. 1440 px holds the presence and the evidence at once, so the running glass arcs around the core on tethers. On the phone it has to stack.
  • History is a drawer on the right, not a scroll that moves the core away. The stage stays live beside it, and a past answer's panels can be replayed into the room. Scroll up over the stage opens the drawer, so the phone's gesture still works.
  • The sidebar recedes while Nova listens, thinks or speaks, and comes back on Ready. A lit edge on the left brings it back on hover. The phone drops its tab bar for the same reason.
  • Keys. Hold ⌥Space to talk and let go to send. Esc stops whatever is happening. ⌘\ opens history. Typing stays in the field.

Restraint

One thing moves at a time: the core on a state change, then the panel that has just been named. The rail, the composer and the drawer use the smooth spring with no overshoot. Only a block landing in the calendar bounces. Under reduced motion every move becomes a cross-fade and the core places without travelling. Under reduced transparency every glass surface is solid.
