What happens

He logs a set. Code computes its estimate and compares it with his best at the resolution it is shown (shownKg). Only if the shown number is higher does the island open: the lift in its muscle's hue, the load large, the estimate counting up from his old best, one haptic tick on the same frame.

Why "a record once you save"

Only the server's check (prsInSession at Save) makes it a record. Until then the words say what is true right now. At Save the plates-stacking moment you chose on 29 Sep plays unchanged; this doesn't replace it.

When it stays quiet

A set that ties his best, a warm-up, a lift with no history, or a bodyweight hold. It opens once per lift per session. It closes after 4 s, or with a swipe up.

Built from

island.js notify({…}), recordKit's shownKg and the e1RM already in trainingAnalytics.js, muscleHue.js for the colour. No server change.

OxeAI: "Weekly active day streak", dots on a line, "2 of 4 days", "0 weeks".

Colour

A done dot fills in the hue of that session's lead muscle group (muscleHue.js), so the week reads as push, pull, legs at a glance. A planned day is an empty ring, today is ringed in ink, and a missed day is dashed. That's the house rule for a gap: never a zero, never red.

The two numbers

2 of 4 counts against the program, so a rest day can't break it. Full weeks in a row counts weeks where every planned session happened, including make-up days. One missed session ends this week, and next week starts clean. A streak counted in days punishes a planned rest.

Make-up days

A make-up session (the makeup-days rule) lifts out of its own day and settles into the dashed slot it finishes. The dashed ring becomes a filled dot, and the week can still be full.

Built from

streaks.js scheduledStreak, the program's planned days, the make-up derivation. Train's momentum tile and Home's line both read one view model.

3 · The day read, both ways

OxeAI: a ring that says 47, then "+ Boosting your score" and "− Hurting your score" in capitals. It never says 47 out of what, or how the 47 is made.

Reasons only (my pick)

Each line is a fact code computed against your own targets: the number, the target, and a small meter with the target marked. The serif line on top is code's sentence, written from the same facts, so it cannot disagree with them. Nothing is a model's guess.

With a number

The same reasons, plus one score from a formula you can open: protein is worth 50 (your share of the floor, capped at 50) and calories are worth 50 (lose a point for every 2% away from target). It is simple and it is honest, but it compresses two different misses into one figure, and you then have to unpack it anyway.

Where a number lies

Flip "Forgot to log dinner". On the left the read says the day is partial and stops judging. On the right a formula sees 900 kcal missing and would score it as a bad day. So the number has to be withheld (dashed ring, "partial"), and on half your days you would get a ring with no number in it.
