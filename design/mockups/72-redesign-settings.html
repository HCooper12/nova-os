What the audit found

Settings today is one scroll with no page of its own for anything: 7.0 phone screens in demo mode (5,913 px at 390 × 844), where 6 of its 18 sections are hidden. Connected, the model board opens all 57 lanes on every launch, which puts the page at about 20 screens (worked out from the code, not measured). 45 controls in demo mode, 30 of them under 44 pt; 12 type sizes; 7 kinds of card; 5 ways of drawing an on and off. Its first screen asks you to connect a vault you connected long ago. The full audit is design/audits/redesign-2026-09/07-settings.md.

The three directions

  • A · Rows. The Index's twin: the you card, then 14 rows in five groups (Nova, Look, Pages, Connected, Under the hood), each with its value on the right and one push to its page. Nothing moves out of Settings.
  • B · Controls. The eight switches you flip sit at the top as lit glass tiles, the way Control Centre draws them; the look is a tile that opens a carousel of complete looks. Everything else is rows.
  • C · Sentence. Nova says how he is set up, in one sentence that code writes from the settings, so the two can never disagree. Each underlined phrase opens its setting. The rest timer and the tab order move to the places you use them.

Fixed in every direction

  • The page opens on Settings, not on “Connect the real vault.” The Mac is one row that says whether it is connected.
  • Talk over Nova shows off, and says why, until “Hey Nova” is on. Today it reads On by default while it cannot run.
  • The tab bar holds four and the page says four, with the line drawn where More begins. Today the copy says three.
  • The page names the six settings that answer to speech. Today it says “most of this page”.
  • Four styles, named as four. Each palette drawn in its own colours; today Observatory's swatch borrows whichever theme is on.
  • Nova is he in every line; six of today's strings still say it.
  • Offline, the rows that come from the Mac show what it last said and when. Today three sections vanish and About you shows as if live.
  • One switch, one picker, and one fill: cyan means on or chosen. The tests are text buttons. Every control is 44 pt.

Colour on these pages

Cyan: on, chosen, and Nova's own. One meaning, where today one cyan fill means on, selected and run this.

Green: a check that passed, a live connection, a suggestion you kept.

Red: an off switch that costs something, a failed check, a restore that overwrites.

Gold: waiting on your call, and nothing else.

Night: the quiet hours on their ring.

Your calls

  1. Which shape. A, B or C, or parts of each (A with B's looks, say). Whatever you pick is drawn again as round 2; a blend is drawn as one page.
  2. Looks or dials. Complete looks you swipe through (B), or style, theme, material and core as four pickers over one live preview (A and C). Looks: one tap per change, with the four pickers a level down. Pickers: every combination one tap away, and you assemble each look yourself.
  3. The tests leave Voice. Can you hear Nova, Can Nova hear you, Nova's ears, the haptics and the swipe facts all move into one Check Nova page. Yes: Voice holds only settings. No: each test stays beside the setting it checks.
  4. C's move. The rest timer goes into Train's live session, and the tab order onto the tab bar itself (hold it, as on the Home Screen, with the Index's Edit as a second door). Yes: Settings holds only what applies everywhere. No: both stay as rows, as in A and B.
  5. Search at the foot. Here it searches the settings (type “quiet” and Quiet hours comes up); the Index's search opens the conversation. Yes: two searches with two jobs. No: it opens the conversation, like the Index.
  6. Text size (decision X3). Nova follows the iPhone's own Text Size, as the Larger switch at the top shows on these pages. Yes: every page takes the same treatment as it is rebuilt. No: sizes stay fixed and X3 stays open.
  7. The fixes that need no design. The Connect title, Talk over, the dock count, the speech claim, “two skins”, the Observatory swatch, Nova as it, and Calm mode's broken Off chip. Say “fix them now” and they go out before round 2; otherwise they ride the build.
