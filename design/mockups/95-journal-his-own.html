Part 2 · Two places

Your page first, the agents one switch away

The page opens to today and the composer. Your entries carry their tag and, quietly, whether they reached Notion. The week's tags sit in one thin bar, so you can see at a glance how much was yours, from your life, or deep.

Own off the cuff Life Nova's life or review prompt Deep a question to think with
Why these three colours

Violet is the Journal's own colour, so your unprompted words wear it. Cyan is Nova's colour: a Life entry began with something Nova drew from your life. Magenta is the hue Nova already gives Technique, the things you think with. Each tag also has its own glyph and word, so colour is never the only signal.

Tagged by where it came from

Typed with no prompt: Own. Answered from a Daily review or My life prompt: Life. Answered from a Deep prompt: Deep. One tap on the tag changes it, with Undo, and the week's bar moves to match.

Quiet when fine, words when not

An entry that reached Notion shows one small glyph. Only the states that need your eye use words: waiting, not connected, written in Notion.

Nova's log is a view

The vault files stay as they are. Mine shows entries whose author is you; the log shows the rest, signed (Nova, Coach, Leader, Guardian, CFO), with Who and Kind in its filter. Kind keeps today's personal, training and system.

Where your words live

As today, in Wiki/Journal/<date>.md, with the tag beside the author in the heading, for example ## 21:10 · personal · by Hayden · deep. That heading is a shared format, so every reader of it changes together. Notion holds the backup.

Part 2 · The prompt

One button, three prompts, use as many as you like

"Give me a prompt" raises one sheet. The three kinds sit side by side behind a three-part switch that follows your swipe. Use one, two or all three: each is answered in turn and saved as its own entry with its own tag.

Deep

Philosophical and psychological questions at the depth of a long-form conversation about how to live. They stand alone: a Deep prompt never reads your schedule, so it never turns into a to-do. The examples in the phone are invented for this mockup and quote no one.

Daily review

Built from the same concept your Home's Daily review shows today, and the shuffle there changes it here too. If you have no concepts yet, the card says so.

My life, with its source named

Drawn from one source Nova really reads: your calendar, training, the Lead picture, money, or the Daily review topic. The source is named under the prompt. A source with nothing readable today (a stale calendar, no money import this month) is drawn dashed and skipped, never guessed.

Another

Each card has its own Another, so changing the Deep question leaves the other two where they are.

What it costs

Today's composer makes one Sonnet call per prompt on the journal-prompt lane. The sheet makes three when it opens, and one more per Another. The skeleton line holds each card's place while its prompt is written.

Part 2 · Notion

Every entry backed up, and Notion entries brought home

Write in Nova and the entry is saved to your vault and then to Notion. Write in Notion and it arrives in your Journal. Four honest states, drawn in order.

How a Nova entry fills the database
  • Entrythe first line of your words
  • Datethe day it belongs to
  • TagOwn, Life or Deep
  • Promptthe prompt you answered, if any
  • Prompt fromNone, Deep question, Daily review or My life
  • Written inNova
  • Nova idties the two copies, so nothing is ever filed twice
  • Createdwhen you wrote it
The order of the hops

Your vault first, Notion second. If your phone cannot reach your Mac, the entry waits on the phone (the outbox Nova already has). If the Mac cannot reach Notion, the entry is safe in your vault and Notion waits. Neither is ever shown as saved before it is.

From Notion

Nova checks the database every few minutes and brings in any page without a Nova id. It lands in your Journal marked Written in Notion, with the Tag you gave it there (Own if you gave none), and gets its Nova id so it never comes back twice.

Undo

Undo on a saved entry removes it from your vault and archives the Notion page (Notion keeps archived pages in its trash for 30 days).

Your calls

Decisions this needs from you

  1. One Journal with a switch, or two Index rows One with a switch (recommended): the Index keeps one Journal row; it always opens to your page and the log is one tap away. Two rows: Journal and Nova's log sit side by side in the Index's Mind group, each its own page.
  2. Magenta for Deep Yes: Deep borrows Technique's magenta, the "things to think with" hue. Another colour: name it, and it replaces magenta on the tag, the bar and the card.
  3. Should Nova's log also go to Notion No (recommended): Notion holds only your own words, which is the backup you asked for. Yes: the agents' entries are copied too, marked with their author, and the Notion database grows by about six a day.
  4. When you edit or delete in Notion Edits follow, deletes don't (recommended): an edit in Notion updates the vault copy; a delete in Notion leaves the vault entry and marks it "removed in Notion". Both follow: a delete in Notion deletes the vault entry too, with Undo in Nova.
  5. When the three prompts are written At the tap: three model calls each time you open the sheet; nothing is spent on days you don't journal. Each morning: the three are ready instantly, but are written (and paid for) every day, journal or not.
  6. The Notion title of an entry Your first line (recommended): the database reads as your words. The prompt: the database reads as a list of questions, with your words inside each page.
  7. The Daily review row on Home after you write Stays with a check (recommended): the row says you wrote on it today. Leaves: the row drops off Home for the rest of the day once you have written on it.
