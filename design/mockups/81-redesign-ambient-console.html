Ambient and Console

The wall should tell a room whether anything waits on you, and Console draws your morning in five instruments. Both are honest, and almost nothing leads to either. Three ways to give them a place.

Demo content: every number, name, event, headline and receipt here is invented. The core, the bodies and the beings are flat stand-ins for the 3D ones in the app.

Today, measured

Demo mode, under your look, with the connected layout drawn from demo-shaped values.

A · A glance

Removes the state washes, the tracked capitals, the nine-second pulse timer and the drawing of the hidden tab-bar orb. Keeps every reading on the wall and all five instruments. Moves Lately (the pulse, the receipts, the streaks) to the wall's second page, and Console onto Home.

Console lives on Home, as a morning card from 05:00 to 11:00, or until you have seen all five. Pin it from Edit to keep it.

B · The day, as a dial

Removes the Console route and one Index row, the state washes, the pulse timer and the hidden orb's drawing. Keeps every reading and all five instruments, nothing said twice. Moves the instruments onto the wall: the 24-hour ring becomes the clock face, and Steps and Protein become instruments under it.

Console lives on the wall, all day, and on the glass when the morning brief is spoken, as it does today.

C · The org

Removes the state washes, the 300-point core (Nova sits at the centre of his agents at 84) and the pulse timer. Keeps every reading, and Console as a screen. Moves the waiting count onto the agents who ask, and gives Console three doors: Home's morning read, an Index row with a value, the wall's controls.

Console lives as its own screen, Your day, reached from Home, the Index and the wall.

In every direction

Fixes that come with whichever you pick.

  • Two touches to leave, back where you were.A tap shows controls; Done, Esc or a swipe down leaves. A bump never ends the wall, and Back never returns to it.
  • Fits upright, on a stand and on the Mac.Nothing sits below the edge; nothing scrolls on a wall.
  • Gold is the only colour that asks.All clear is quiet ink. No signal is grey, with words and a dashed outline.
  • The core costs less.Smaller, 30 frames a second, a still frame when dimmed; the tab bar's orb stops drawing under the wall. The whole wall drifts a few points an hour, clock and core included.
  • Reduced motion means still.The colon holds, the pulse stops rising, the instruments fade in.
  • Console speaks, and always has a way forward.A VoiceOver label for every bar, block and band; Read again in the empty and error states; a skeleton while it reads; Home's colours.

Your calls

  1. C1: where Console lives.A puts it on Home as a morning card, and the route and its Index row go. B retires it into the wall, which then gets a Mac sidebar row and one Index row instead of two. C keeps it as its own screen, Your day, with three doors. Either way the five instruments keep appearing on the glass when the morning brief is spoken.
  2. Which wall: A, B or C.A is the simplest and closest to StandBy; B says your day in one look; C makes the wall your agents, as you agreed in principle on 25 September, and is the most work. The wall and the Console answer can mix, for example A's wall with C's screen.
  3. All clear: no colour, or keep the cyan.My pick is no colour, so gold is the only colour on the wall that means anything. Keeping cyan keeps today's look, but cyan is also Nova thinking.
  4. The core on the wall: small and capped, or full size.Small, at 30 frames a second, is my pick: it cuts most of the wall's drawing over an evening. Full size keeps today's big presence and its cost.
  5. Night.Dim the wall to about half from 22:00 to 06:00 with the core still, or leave brightness to iOS. No red night tint either way: red stays Nova pushing back.
  6. "Hey Nova" on the wall.Today the wake word switches off while the wall is up. Allowing it, when you have it on, lets you talk to the wall from across the room; leaving it off keeps the wall's microphone closed.
