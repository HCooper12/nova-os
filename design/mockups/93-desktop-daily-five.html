The daily five, made for your MacBook

Home, Nova, Train, Fuel and Inbox, drawn three ways for a 1512 × 982 screen. Today each is the phone page centred, with 40% of the width left empty; these use it.

Demo content. Every name, number, time, recipe, bill and message below is invented for this mockup. Nova's replies in the frames are scripted. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Every frame works. Click inside one, then press ? for its keys. A click on the rail or sidebar blurs through to the next page; ⌥1 to ⌥5 cut straight to it.

The same map in all three directions. Shortcuts avoid ⌘1 to ⌘9, which Safari and Chrome keep for their own tabs, and every single-letter key works only outside a text field.
