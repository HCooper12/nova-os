its knowledge and context about him and the information it holds. Some of the

engagement is evidence about the design. See nova-produce-vs-keep (154 drafts a
month, 9 kept) and the context ledger, NOVA-METHOD.md §5.

Suggested first step: a read-only audit that measures the four questions above
from the conversation record, inbox history and agent contexts, using the
"ask it yourself" method, before anything is built.
