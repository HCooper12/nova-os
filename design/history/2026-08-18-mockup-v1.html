THE THESIS

Readiness is the hero. The first thing every morning is the ring — recovery, block week, today's card — then one glowing action. No scrolling to find out what today is.

MOMENTUM FEED

PRs (gold), plateaus (warn), streaks (green) as swipeable cards — the analytics engine made visible. Each card is a doorway: tap the plateau, land in Coach with context loaded.

LOGGER AS COCKPIT

Set pills carry RPE/RIR inline; set-type and pain are one tap, not a form. Coach's focus prescription and earned progressions sit on the exercise row where the decision happens.

COACH AS SURFACE

Curated resources render as playable cards, proposals as approve/leave chips. The chat stops being a footnote and becomes the third tab.

ONE TRUTH, ONE RING

Protein-to-floor is the number, so it owns the hero — with kcal, C/F, and the "fits what's left" budget beside it. The Coach's one-liner makes it a plan, not a stat.

THE WEEK IS HONEST

Your real last-7: gaps stay visibly thin, the floor is a line you either crossed or didn't, today glows cyan because it's still moving.

EATING IS ONE TAP

Rotation meals as swipeable cards with a satisfying tick — the thing you do 4× a day gets the biggest targets on the screen (44 px+).

LOG ANYTHING, FAST

One bar: type it, shoot it, or scan it. Retro days and editing live one tap deeper — visible, not buried.

WHAT SHIPS FIRST

Phase 1: the Train Today pane (readiness ring, block chip, today card, momentum feed) and the Fuel hero + week strip — highest feel-per-effort, all data already served. Phase 2: the session logger cockpit. Phase 3: Coach-as-tab. Same tokens as the live app throughout — this is Nova, sharpened.
