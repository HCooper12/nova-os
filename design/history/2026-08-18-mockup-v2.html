FOCUS FOR TODAY

Composed from the day's routine + earned progressions + Coach focuses + block week. Rest days get a real mobility/recovery focus. Never filler — if nothing meaningful exists, the card doesn't render.

JARGON EXPLAINS ITSELF

Every term of art is dotted-underlined — tap RIR or ACCUMULATION in the phone. One reusable glossary sheet; if a term can't earn a plain-English line, it doesn't ship.

NOTES + ANOMALY

Every exercise takes a free note; ANOMALY excludes today from progression/plateau signals ("off day, don't learn from this"). Coach digests notes over time — pull-ups trending easy is how "go weighted" gets proposed.

THE PAIN FLOW

Two taps (where/when) → evidence-based triage: stop, stretch, or same-muscle substitute — and one tap files it to the Injury Log Coach reads before every prescription.

DECLINE ASKS WHY

A declined proposal triggers the why — and Coach argues back when the data disagrees. Supportive, never sycophantic.

VOLUME vs YOUR GOALS

Target ticks on each muscle bar; under-volumed goal muscles flag red with a one-tap fix. Arms goal = tris/bis watched.

FORM ON EVERY LIFT

▶ FORM on each exercise opens the curated clip/diagram + variations, with Coach recommending the variation for YOUR goals.
